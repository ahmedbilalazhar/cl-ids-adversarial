# Second implementation and evaluation gap audit

24 October 2026. This extends G01–G31 in [implementation-gap-audit.md](implementation-gap-audit.md). Evidence comes from source inspection, new full-source data censuses, exact design arithmetic and primary literature. No Phase 3 model was trained or tested. A documented fix is not an implemented learner fix.

## Assessment

The study is **not ready for final experiments**. The research questions are specific, and the plan already contains useful paired controls. The largest remaining gap is the absence of a conforming implementation and its acceptance evidence. The second largest is data feasibility: a plausible download and millions of rows do not ensure the required isolated family support. More GPU accounts do not resolve either issue.

The present evidence supports a detailed implementation proposal. It does not support claims that the defense works, that the study is reproduced, or that publication quality has been achieved.

## What was actually improved in this audit

The exact allocator and data-order proposal, evaluation interpretation, design-resolution calculation, resource/message specification and repository entry-point text are concrete changes. Full public archives and source censuses provide new **data-feasibility evidence**. They do not close the learner conformance gaps, establish the final split or supply experimental performance.

## Evidence gates, in order

1. **Data:** accepted provenance/schema/clock/label decisions, reason-coded census, complete component graph, T0 isolation, split/support and benign-pool feasibility.
2. **Reference engine:** growing registry, correct immutable broadcasts/reset, corruption/admission semantics, replay and importance state.
3. **Acceptance/recovery:** cross-reviewed evidence for state ordering, no held-out access, durable exact continuation and artifact import.
4. **Development:** clean learning/numerics, explicit false-alarm reporting, category-specific profiling, selected settings and scope.
5. **Freeze:** complete scientific/runtime identities, primary/exploratory labels, hypothesis/missingness policy and selected paired matrix.
6. **Final evidence:** compatible complete runs or visible failure dispositions; sealed evaluation; reproducible plots/counts/contrasts.
7. **Publication:** informative specific finding, fair closest-method comparison, calibrated limitations and actual external critique/reproduction where claimed.

Do not count planning-document checks as passage of gates 1–7. The next useful implementation milestone is one correct small reference trajectory on an approved data contract, followed by the authors' acceptance work; it is not a larger final run grid.
