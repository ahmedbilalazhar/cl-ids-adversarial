## Phase 2 methodological corrections

The accompanying document revision also addresses thirteen issues:

1. T0 isolation now covers every supervised experience and partition.
2. Sessions spanning different attack experiences are quarantined before allocation to prevent early exposure of future classes.
3. The scaler, T0-constant feature policy, future out-of-range reporting and transformed-finiteness audit are explicit proposed choices.
4. Canonical duplicate representatives prevent identical training content occupying multiple replay slots under different IDs; post-transform collisions are rechecked.
5. Replay timing distinguishes later same-experience exposure from replay after attacker withdrawal; stored teachers are not silently refreshed by aggregation.
6. Inactive clients preserve importance/anchor state and do not contribute updates.
7. Statistical contrasts have declared directions and whole-vector seed pairing.
8. Mean bootstrap/sign-flip intervals are distinguished from signed-rank location tests and their assumptions.
9. The unspecified overflow comparator is defined as unique-ID FIFO.
10. Class-balanced eviction has a concrete rule and does not claim lifetime-uniform reservoir sampling.
11. Latency and communication measurement have repeatable proposed procedures rather than inferred deployment claims.
12. The external protocol requires its own pre-outcome schedule/source manifest, or it will be omitted.
13. Favorable security/adaptation endpoint directions are stated individually; slash notation no longer suggests ratios or automatic joint benefit.

All corrections remain proposed protocol decisions. Reported research-paper results remain attributed to their authors. No implementation status or project performance is presented in the submission.
